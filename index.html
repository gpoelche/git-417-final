How Do I Earn Points?

All purchases of physical and/or digital software, amiibo and eligible merchandise through the Nintendo eShop will automatically deposit earned points to your MyNintendo account. Members will receive three points for every one dollar they spend in each transaction.

These points can be redeemed for exclusive items within the Club Nintendo Member's Shop! Points may also be redeemed as credit towards a purchase made through the Nintendo eShop (one dollar is redeemed for every fifteen points earned).

Earn Exclusive Drops with Club Nintendo Ambassadors

Nintendo is proud to announce that players can earn more with their Club Nintendo membership by tuning to content creators who've partnered with us as Club Nintendo Ambassadors!

By linking various streaming and/or entertainment accounts with a MyNintendo account, players can be eligible for receiving exlusive rewards when watching content from a Club Nintendo Ambassador. Drops from watching Ambassadors and their content may include bonus points, limited-time icons and borders for your MyNintendo profile, discounts on select software titles and more.
